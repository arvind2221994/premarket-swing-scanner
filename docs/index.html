Trading glossary

Plain-language definitions for dashboard signals

PCR (put-call ratio)
Compares put and call open interest near the current price. It helps describe options positioning, but is not a buy or sell signal by itself.
OI (open interest)
The number of open derivatives contracts. Price and OI together can hint whether traders are adding or closing positions.
ATR (average true range)
A measure of normal daily price movement. The scanner uses it to spot entries that may already be overextended.
Slippage
The estimated difference between the expected trade price and the likely execution price. Lower is generally better.
Sector-relative strength
How the stock performed versus its sector over five sessions. Positive values favor bullish setups; negative values favor bearish setups.
